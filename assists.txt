## 💡 PRO TIPS

- For the **icon**: Generate at 1024×1024, then resize to 800×800 in Canva free
- For the **banner**: Generate at 1792×1024 (closest free ratio), upscale with upscayl.org (free, open source)
- Add the actual text **"TinyToons Universe"** in Canva after generating — AI text is often blurry
- Use **Fredoka One** font (free Google Font) for all text overlays to match your brand
- **Color codes to use in Canva**: Primary `#4F46E5`, Accent `#D97706`, Background dark `#0F172A`
- Generate 3–4 variants of each and pick the best one
